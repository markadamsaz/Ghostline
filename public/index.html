How to play

  1. Set the zoneThe host draws a play area on the map and shares the 4-letter code.
  2. HideOne player is picked to seek. Everyone else gets a head start to hide inside the zone.
  3. Survive the pingsEvery few minutes each hider's phone shows the seekers a rough spot. Keep moving.
  4. Tag or escapeSeekers tag hiders by getting close, an honor tag, or a photo. Last hiders standing win.
HidersShown in black. Stay hidden, stay in the zone.
SeekersShown in red. Follow the pings and tag.

The host can add game modes like Infection, Jailbreak, Supply drops and a Shrinking zone. Play somewhere safe and public, stay off private property, and watch for traffic. Your location is only shared with players in your game while it runs, and nothing is stored.
