How it works
  1. The host draws a play zone on the map and shares the 4-letter code.
  2. One player is picked at random to seek. Everyone else gets a head start to hide.
  3. Every few minutes, each hider's phone pings their spot to the seekers. Fuzzy, but close.
  4. Pick how tags work: GPS range, honor tag (they confirm), or photo tag (snap them, they confirm). The host settles arguments.
  5. Mix and match game modes: Infection, Jailbreak, Fugitive, Supply drops, Missions, Capture points, Hot potato, Final sprint and more.
  6. The zone closes in over the round. Step out and your phone screams at you. Stay out 15 seconds and you're out.
  7. Power-ups like decoy pings, jammers, sonar and radar. Supply drops hold super ones, but everyone can see them.

Play somewhere safe and public, stay off private property, and watch for traffic. Your location is only shared with players in your game while it runs, and nothing is stored.
